An Incomplete Risk Analysis Creates Blind Spots</strong></span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">A HIPAA risk analysis helps organizations identify where ePHI exists, how it moves through systems, and what threats or vulnerabilities could compromise its confidentiality, integrity, and availability.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">When healthcare organizations acquire another practice, introduce new software, or connect third-party systems, their security environment changes.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">LVHN's incident demonstrates why acquired organizations cannot remain outside the enterprise security assessment process.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">Healthcare leaders should maintain an updated inventory of systems, applications, vendors, and ePHI repositories. Collecting Audit Logs Is Not Enough</strong></span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">Security systems may generate thousands of activity records, but those records provide limited protection if nobody reviews them.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">Organizations need procedures for examining audit logs, access reports, and security incident records to identify suspicious activity.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">Assign clear ownership, establish review schedules, investigate unusual access patterns, and document corrective actions.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">The important distinction is simple: security monitoring must lead to meaningful action, not just data collection.</span></p><h2 dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;"><strong>5 HIPAA Cybersecurity Priorities for Healthcare Executives</strong></span></h2><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;">The LVHN settlement offers five practical lessons for healthcare leadership.</span></p><p dir="ltr"><span style="font-family: 'book antiqua', palatino, serif;"><strong>1.
